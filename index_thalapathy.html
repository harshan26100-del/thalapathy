THE ICON

Joseph Vijay Chandrasekhar

Born June 22, 1974 in Chennai, Vijay redefined Tamil commercial cinema through effortless charismatic screen presence, iconic dance choreography, and unmatched box office loyalty.

34
Years of Glory

Making his lead acting debut in 1992's Naalaiya Theerpu directed by his father S. A. Chandrasekhar, Vijay quickly emerged as the beloved family hero with romantic blockbusters like Poove Unakkaga (1996) and Kadhalukku Mariyadhai (1997).

The 2000s cemented his title as "Thalapathy" (Commander) through all-time mass blockbusters Ghilli (2004) — the first Tamil film to break ₹50 Crores domestically — and Pokkiri (2007).

From 2012 onwards, Vijay spearheaded modern pan-Indian cinema with Thuppakki, Kaththi, Mersal, Master, and Leo, establishing an unprecedented streak of 8 consecutive ₹200-Crore-plus worldwide grossers.
